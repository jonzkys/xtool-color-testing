**Loom** is a new page that replaces the old *SVG stack* tab. Where XCS
Studio already handles fixed-pass stack copies, Loom does something it
can't: fill a shape with hatch lines whose laser parameters **drift
across the artwork**.

## What it does

- Drop an SVG *or* a PNG/JPG (traced to a silhouette in-browser). The
  whole artwork is treated as one shape — no layer detection.
- Pick a parameter to ramp (power, speed, frequency, density, passes,
  pulse width, or spacing).
- Pick the axis the ramp runs along — perpendicular to the hatch, along
  the hatch, pure X, or pure Y.
- Place as many stops on the ramp as you need; the backend interpolates
  piecewise-linearly between them at burn time.

## How to read the preview

The hatch lines are coloured with a **bone-white → golden-ember →
Loom-orange → charred → near-black** ramp based on burn intensity at
each point, and each line's thickness scales with the same intensity.
The strip below the preview shows the exact colour-for-value mapping so
you can verify a ramp visually before spending ten minutes on a burn.

Scrub the slider to reveal the hatch progressively, or hit play for an
auto-scrub so you can see how the gradient phases in line by line.
