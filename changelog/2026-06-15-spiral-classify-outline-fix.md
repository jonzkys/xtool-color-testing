title: Spiral Cut — every body cuts as external; split-detail keeps the outline whole
summary: Free-standing bodies — the separate letters of a name, a scatter of flowers — now cut as "external" (pink) like the main silhouette; only true holes and counters stay "internal" (amber). Previously just the single largest body was external, so loose letters were mis-tinted and cut as detail. And turning on "Split internal detail" no longer gouges the silhouette — the part contour is always traced in full, so the outline stays continuous.
